# Third-party notices

## On-Scroll 3D Carousel, Codrops

The `ring` behaviour in src/runtime.js is rebuilt, without GSAP, after
Codrops' On-Scroll 3D Carousel (https://tympanus.net/codrops/?p=93330): the
cards standing around a circle, the scroll turning and tilting it and
lighting the cards, the scroll's speed spreading and leaning them on a
spring, and a drag that spins it with a little inertia.
